HOW TO VIEW THE FAMILY TREE PRESENTATION
=========================================

1. Open the "family-tree" folder on your computer.

2. Double-click the file named "index.html" — it will open automatically
   in your default web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge all work).

3. The presentation will start playing on its own.

CONTROLS
--------
  Space bar       — Play / Pause
  Right arrow (→) — Go to the next slide
  Left arrow  (←) — Go to the previous slide

  You can also use the buttons at the bottom of the screen.

NOTE: No internet connection is needed. Everything runs locally on your computer.
